## What I Do

Facilitate data-driven marketing retrospectives that go beyond "what happened" to understand WHY it happened and what to do differently. Output is a structured report with concrete next-iteration recommendations — not a list of observations.

## The Retrospective Framework

### Section 1: Campaign Summary (The Facts)
- Campaign name, dates, objective
- Target audience
- Channels used
- Budget spent
- Key results vs. targets (per KPI)

### Section 2: What Worked (Specific)
- Which specific elements outperformed? (specific ads, emails, audiences — not "the campaign")
- What was the #1 driver of success?
- Which hypothesis was validated?

### Section 3: What Didn't Work (Honest)
- What underperformed vs. expectations?
- Where did the audience drop off in the funnel?
- What assumption turned out to be wrong?

### Section 4: Root Cause Analysis
For each underperformance:
- Was it a **targeting** problem, **messaging** problem, **timing** problem, or **offer** problem?
- Apply the 5 Whys framework to find the real root cause — not the surface symptom

### Section 5: Learnings to Systemize
- What should become a repeatable process or template?
- What creative angle should be tested next?
- What audience segment surprised us and deserves more investment?

### Section 6: Next Iteration Recommendations
- The ONE change with the biggest positive impact
- 3 specific hypotheses to test in the next campaign
- What to **kill**, **keep**, or **scale**

## Process

```
1. INGEST    -> Receive campaign summary + data/results
2. ANALYZE   -> Segment by channel, creative, audience — never average mixed results
3. ROOT CAUSE -> Apply 5 Whys to each underperformance
4. SYSTEMIZE -> Extract what becomes a template vs. a one-off
5. OUTPUT    -> Structured report with 3-5 concrete actions
```

## How to Trigger

```
"Run a marketing retrospective on this campaign.
Results: [paste data/summary].
What do we double down on? What do we kill? What are the 3 hypotheses to test next?"
```

## Rules

- No vanity metric celebration — revenue, pipeline, or retention impact only
- Every "what didn't work" must have a root cause, not just a description
- Every recommendation must be specific enough that a new team member could act on it
- Mixed results get segmented — never summarized as "average performance"
- Output must include 3-5 concrete actions, not a list of observations

## Edge Cases

**No data, only qualitative observations**: Work with qualitative signals but flag that recommendations are directional, not conclusive.

**Mixed results**: Segment by channel, creative, or audience. Mixed ≠ average.

**Team in defensive mode**: Frame around "what we learned" not "what failed." Goal is learning, not blame.
